About PDB Expo

Connecting the Global PDR Industry. Advancing Innovation. Creating Opportunities.

More Than an Exhibition

December 17–19, 2027 Manila, Philippines

PDB Expo International Summit & Expo is a premier international gathering dedicated to advancing the paintless dent repair (PDR) industry and connecting the broader automotive appearance and surface-care community.

Taking place December 17–19, 2027, in Manila, Philippines, this three-day event brings together PDR professionals, tool manufacturers, automotive detailing specialists, industry innovators, entrepreneurs, and business leaders from around the world.

More than an exhibition, PDB Expo is a platform where technical excellence meets business opportunity—bringing competition, education, innovation, and professional connections together under one roof.

One International Event. Multiple Opportunities.

International PDR Championship

Where precision, skill, and excellence take center stage.

The International PDR Championship is a signature highlight of PDB Expo, bringing together talented paintless dent repair technicians to demonstrate their craftsmanship, technical expertise, and competitive spirit.

The championship celebrates professional excellence while inspiring the next generation of technicians and promoting the continued advancement of PDR worldwide.

Global Exhibitions & Industry Innovation

Discover the tools, technologies, and products shaping the future.

Explore an international showcase featuring PDR tools and equipment, automotive detailing products, paint and surface-care innovations, and emerging technologies.

PDB Expo provides manufacturers, distributors, suppliers, and service providers with opportunities to introduce their products, connect with industry professionals, expand their visibility, and explore new markets across Asia-Pacific and beyond.

Business Seminars & AI Workshops

Beyond technical skills. Building stronger businesses.

The automotive industry is evolving, and business success requires more than technical expertise. Our educational program is designed to help professionals, entrepreneurs, and business owners strengthen their operations, explore emerging technologies, and identify new growth opportunities.

Topics may include:

  • Business development, entrepreneurship, and strategic growth
  • Marketing, branding, and customer acquisition
  • Artificial intelligence and practical business applications
  • AI-powered automation, productivity, and customer engagement
  • Digital transformation and emerging industry trends
  • Professional development and operational excellence

Through expert-led seminars, interactive workshops, and industry discussions, participants can gain practical knowledge to help them adapt, compete, and grow in a changing business environment.

International Networking & Collaboration

Building relationships that extend beyond the event.

PDB Expo creates opportunities for meaningful connections among industry professionals, manufacturers, distributors, educators, and business leaders.

Whether exploring distribution partnerships, discovering new technologies, exchanging expertise, or developing international relationships, attendees can engage with a global community committed to professional and industry growth.

Our Vision

To establish PDB Expo as a recognized international platform that advances professional excellence, encourages innovation, strengthens industry collaboration, and creates meaningful business opportunities throughout Asia-Pacific and the global automotive community.

Our Mission

To connect industry professionals, manufacturers, innovators, and entrepreneurs through international competition, exhibitions, education, and business networking—supporting the growth and evolution of the paintless dent repair and automotive appearance industries.

Why Manila, Philippines?

A destination for international connection.

Located in Southeast Asia, Manila provides a dynamic setting for bringing together automotive professionals and businesses from across Asia-Pacific and international markets.

Hosting PDB Expo in the Philippines creates an opportunity to connect regional talent with global expertise, introduce new products and technologies, and encourage cross-border industry collaboration.

Be Part of the Global Connection

Whether you are a technician, manufacturer, distributor, entrepreneur, educator, or automotive enthusiast, PDB Expo offers an opportunity to discover, connect, learn, and grow.
